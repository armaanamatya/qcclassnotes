\textbf{Weekly Exam Packets for Midterm 1} (lectures of August 25 -- October 8, 2026)

\vspace{0.1in}

\hrule

\vspace{0.1in}

One section per week. Read it after that week's review session. Each section starts with the
results to have open in the scribes, then gives likely questions with full solutions. The
questions are a guess at the exam, not questions from the professor: they take the exercises
assigned in class and change the function, the numbers or one step. Every solution says which
scribe results it uses. Numbers such as ``Theorem 6, p.~8'' refer to the scribes as submitted
(\texttt{submission/Amatya\_Scribes1.pdf}), the only copy allowed in the exam. A week only uses material up to that
week. The few places that go beyond the scribes are marked.
